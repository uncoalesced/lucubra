# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the Lucubra project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Accepting responsibility, apologising to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience.
- Focusing on what is best for the community as a whole.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- The use of sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Sharing other people's study material, grades or personal records in an issue, pull request or discussion.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behaviour they judge inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including the repository, its issues and pull requests, and any official discussion channel. It also applies when an individual represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour through GitHub. Send a private message to the maintainer through the maintainer's GitHub profile at `https://github.com/uncoalesced`, or use GitHub's private reporting for the repository where one is available. Do not post the report in a public issue. Reports are reviewed by the maintainers. Every report is handled with confidentiality, to the extent that the situation allows.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers follow these guidelines when deciding on consequences:

1. **Correction.** A private, written warning with a clear explanation of the problem and a request for changed behaviour. No further consequences are needed.
2. **Warning.** A warning with consequences for continued behaviour. A temporary restriction on interaction with the community may apply.
3. **Temporary ban.** A temporary ban from interaction with the project for a set period, after sustained inappropriate behaviour.
4. **Permanent ban.** A permanent ban from the project after a pattern of violations, or a single severe violation.
